Selected Work

Oxford University WildCRU

I built the WildMAPS Species Distribution Model dashboard. This catalog of raster data is a research tool for ecologists worldwide. It's built to display big files (100s of gigabytes) smoothly and with very low hosting cost (pennies per week). Behind the scenes, the researchers can use the workflow I built to keep the website updated.

Tools: rasterio, shapely, geopandas, GDAL, tippecanoe, AWS S3, Maplibre-GL JS, React, OpenStreetMap

NHS psychiatrists

I processed more than five million rows of mental-health reporting, for a group of NHS (UK National Health Service) psychiatrists trying to understand how different restraint methods are used. The results and black-and-white visualisations revealed severe gaps in reporting, and were published in BJPsych Bulletin.

Tools: Matplotlib (PyPlot), NumPy, xarray, Excel

MapAction

Since joining UK-based charity MapAction as a geospatial volunteer, I've spent over seven weeks working emergency humanitarian responses. This has included field missions to the 2025 Myanmar earthquake and 2026 floods in southern Mozambique. Under extreme time pressure, we produce maps and manage information for all stages of the response: Search and Rescue, Rapid Needs Assessment, and Distribution. We make use of all sorts of public datasets: OpenStreetMap, gridded population, building extents, and more.

Tools: ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, Python, OpenStreetMap
